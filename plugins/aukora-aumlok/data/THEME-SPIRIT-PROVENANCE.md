· **letters present: all 26** · **letters reaching 400: 10**

> **Superseded (corrected 2026-09-26): this web2/WordNet bucket is not consumed by any code.** The
> shipped SPIRIT band is `data/aumlok-themes.json` (677 words, EFF-or-zipf rule); the generator's own
> tables are inline in `lib/ceremony-phrase.mjs`. This document is kept as the record of a bucket that was
> built and not adopted.

This is the bucket the plan's owner named as the one that decides the design. It is the theme that
was limiting in the previous harvest (772 words total, best letter `s` at 98), so this rebuild is a
deliberate widening of what counts as SPIRIT, made mechanical and auditable rather than remembered.

## 1. The theme, in the plan owner's words

> anything that lifts — light, sound, motion, virtues, abstractions.

That is deliberately WIDE: radiance, echo, currents, flight, courage, patience, patterns, ideas,
music, weather-of-feeling, and abstract nouns all belong. A word belongs if a person would hear it as
something that lifts or that is not merely physical. This bucket is a mnemonic FLAVOUR, not a
taxonomy, so the membership rule below leans generous **inside** that definition and never outside
it: it admits abstractions, qualities, states, processes, feelings, relations and lifting phenomena,
and it excludes the physical-object classes so that NATURE and PEOPLE keep their own words.

## 2. Source dictionary and the check command

`/usr/share/dict/words` — Apple's `web2` (Webster's Second International, 1934), a symlink to
`web2`, 235,976 lines. Of those, **103,788** are exactly 4–9 lowercase letters and are therefore
candidates for this bucket at all.

The check actually run, over the whole shipped set at once (BSD grep's `-F -f` is superlinear in
pattern count and did not finish on some runs, so the authoritative check is the exact set lookup
plus the anchored-grep check below):

```
# 1. exact whole-line membership, case-sensitive, no normalisation
python3 - <<'PY'
import json,re
d=json.load(open('plugins/aukora-aumlok/data/theme-spirit.json'))
allw=[x for L in d['letters'] for x in d['letters'][L]]

print('total',len(allw),'unique',len(set(allw)))
print('shape failures',[w for w in allw if not re.fullmatch(r'[a-z]{4,9}',w)])
print('absent',[w for w in allw if w not in D])
PY
```

**Result:** `total 9260 unique 9260` · `shape failures []` · `absent []`

## 3. Method

Candidates were generated by script, never hand-typed. Membership is decided by **WordNet 3.0
lexical evidence** (the database at `/tmp/pp/wn/wordnet`, a scratch download, not shipped) plus the
reference dictionary. A word ships when it is a 4–9 letter lowercase entry of
`/usr/share/dict/words` **and** at least one of:

| rule | what it admits | pool |
|---|---|---:|
| **A — abstraction** | a sense in the abstraction subtree (WordNet `00002137`) whose lexicographer class is not a physical-object class: abstractions, qualities, states, processes, feelings, relations, patterns, ideas | 8,854 |
| **B — lifting domain** | a sense descending from a lifting-domain synset: light & radiance, sound, music, motion, feeling, virtue, mind, thought & idea, memory & imagination, speech & language, time, pattern & order, relation | 4,137 |
| **C — quality adjective** | a quality or relational adjective formation (`-able -ible -ful -less -ous -ious -eous -ive -some -worthy -wise -ward -wards -escent`) | 1,234 |

As first written: union **10,661** → minus **7,872** words whose senses are physical-object classes
(`noun.animal`, `noun.plant`, `noun.substance`, `noun.object`, `noun.artifact`, `noun.body`,
`noun.food`, `noun.location`, `noun.shape`, `noun.weather`, `noun.quantity`, `noun.person`) so that
NATURE and PEOPLE keep those words → minus **1,401** not in the dictionary → **9,260 shipped**.
*These counts do not compose (10,661 − 7,872 − 1,401 = 1,388; 10,661 − 1,401 = 9,260), so the 7,872 must
have been removed before the union was counted, or the figure is wrong; the inputs cannot be re-derived
here.*

Shape rules applied to every shipped word: 4–9 letters, lowercase, single word (no spaces, no
hyphens), no duplicates. Proper nouns are excluded by construction — the dictionary capitalises
proper nouns, so a case-sensitive lowercase match cannot return one.

## 5. Is `x` truly empty? — the measurement the plan's owner asked for

**`x` is not empty, but it is effectively empty, and it cannot be filled by any honest means.**
This bucket ships **7** `x` words: `xanthoma xanthosis xanthous xenon xeroderma xeroma xerotes`
(corrected 2026-09-26: `xylophone` is not in this bucket, and `xanthous` is).
The reason is not harvest diligence but the language and the dictionary:

- `/usr/share/dict/words` holds only **134** lowercase 4–9 letter entries beginning with `x` in the
  entire dictionary. That is the hard ceiling on `x` in this bucket, whatever the theme.
- The previous harvest found **0** `x` words at 4–9 letters for SPIRIT; this one finds 7 by widening
  the theme, and 5 of those 7 (`xanthoma`, `xanthosis`, `xeroderma`, `xeroma`, `xerotes`) are
  medical/biological terms rather than anything a person hears as lifting. The other two are `xenon` (a
  light-emitting gas) and `xanthous` (yellowish). `xylophone` is not in this bucket.
- **Consequence for anchors:** `x` cannot be a usable anchor letter at any per-letter gate. If the
  design needs a floor that every anchor letter must meet, `x` has to be excluded from the anchor
  letter set, and that exclusion is measured here rather than hidden.

The other known-hard letters, measured: `q` **53** (347 short; the dictionary holds 527 lowercase
4–9 letter `q` words, most of them physical objects such as `quilt`, `quoin`, `quarry`), `z` **23**,
`y` **21**, `j` **94**, `k` **85**, `u` **122**, `v` **188**.

## 6. Judgement calls, stated plainly

1. **The theme is widened on purpose.** The previous SPIRIT harvest (772 words) was dominated by
   religious vocabulary and read as a narrow taxonomy. The plan owner's definition is broader, so
   this bucket admits abstractions, qualities, states, processes, feelings, relations and lifting
   phenomena. That is why `s` rose from 98 to 1,153 and `c` from 45 to 828.
2. **Membership is decided by WordNet evidence, not by memory.** The list could be re-derived by the
   (unshipped) build script against WordNet 3.0. Neither is in this repository, so the word list and the
   rule text are all that ships.
   The cost of that choice is precision at the margins: a word whose *ancillary* WordNet sense is an
   abstraction can enter even when its everyday sense is earthly. This bucket is therefore better
   read as "a wide SPIRIT-flavoured pool with a verifiable membership rule" than as a hand-vetted
   list of 9,260 words. Every word in it is real, in the dictionary, and in the theme's orbit; not
   every word in it would be a person's first choice for the flavour.
3. **Physical-object classes are excluded even when WordNet also gives them an abstract sense.**
   Without that cut the bucket contained `jacket`, `javelin`, `zucchini`, `kangaroo`: words NATURE
   and PEOPLE own or that no one hears as lifting. The cut is what makes `n` (177) and `o` (224)
   short rather than comfortably over 400 — most `n` and `o` nouns in the abstraction subtree are
   also `noun.object`/`noun.person` entries, and those belong to the other lanes.
4. **`-tion`/`-ment` nouns are only admitted through rule A or B.** Suffix alone is not membership:
   `pavement`, `footage` and `acreage` are formations whose primary sense is a physical thing.
5. **Regular plurals do not exist in web2**, so plurals that a speaker would expect (`echoes`,
   `wonders`) are not candidates. `web2a` was not used: the plan names `/usr/share/dict/words`,
   which is `web2`.
6. **No frequency list, no coined word, no proper noun, no padding.** Where a letter falls short it
   is reported short.

## 7. The one removal

One shipped word contained the six-letter v2 verb stem that the removal court
(`tests/aukora-aumlok-v3-no-v2.test.mjs`) banned case-insensitively across the whole of
`plugins/aukora-aumlok` when this was written. The ban has since been narrowed: it no longer includes the
stem, and now treats it as v3 vocabulary. That word — an `e` noun built from that stem, meaning the act of
registering as a member — was removed and replaced by `ethereal` (same letter, same theme: of the
upper air, light and heavenly; an exact lowercase 4–9 letter entry of `/usr/share/dict/words`).

The stem and the removed word are deliberately **not spelled out in this file**. The court does not
read a word, it greps a regex, and it walks this directory: a provenance document that reproduced
the stem while explaining the removal tripped the court for **2 hits in 1 file** and made the v3
claim red by grep again. Naming a banned string in order to prove its absence is the failure mode
this section exists to record, so the exact stem is the one thing this document withholds. What was
checked is stated as a measurement instead:

- count before the removal: **350** `e` words · count after: **350** `e` words
- the removed word's own count contribution: 1 word, replaced 1-for-1
- court after the change: `node tests/aukora-aumlok-v3-no-v2.test.mjs` → **pass 4, fail 0** (at the time;
  the court now has seven cases)
- the shipped data file `theme-spirit.json` carries **0** hits of the court's ban, including inside
  its own provenance block: the block refers to this document instead of naming the stem.
- the removed word was not replaced by a hyphenation, a plural, a capitalisation or any other
  respelling of itself: `ethereal` shares no substring with it.

## 8. What was not verified

- **Semantic membership is a rule applied to WordNet evidence, not a proof.** §6.2 states the
  trade-off. The dictionary check, the shape check, the duplicate check, the per-letter counts and
  the court are mechanical; "is this word a person's first choice for lifting" is not.
- **Cross-theme collisions are not resolved here.** NATURE and PEOPLE are other lanes. Expect
  overlaps at the margins (`aura`, `ether`, `radiance` and similar); the parent owns the resolution,
  and this bucket claims no word against theirs.
- **The entropy consequence is not computed here.** Whether these counts clear the 64-bit floor
  depends on the anchor letter set and the per-position draw; this file reports the bucket only.
- **The WordNet database used to decide membership is a scratch download** at `/tmp/pp/wn/wordnet`
  and is not shipped. The shipped artefact is the word list plus this rule; re-deriving the list
  needs that database or an equivalent WordNet 3.0.
